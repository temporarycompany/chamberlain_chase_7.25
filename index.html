John Chamberlain took the crushed, painted steel of the American automobile — the most ordinary material of postwar life — and welded it into some of the most consequential sculpture of the 20th century, translating the gesture of Abstract Expressionist painting into three dimensions.

April 16, 2027 marks 100 years since his birth. It is a moment the art world will mark with or without a brand partner — the opportunity is to be inside that conversation from day one, authentically, as the institution that helped bring it to a wider audience.

“I believe that common materials are the best materials.”

— John Chamberlain
Credentials
Collections. Permanent holdings at the Guggenheim, Whitney Museum of American Art, MoMA, Tate Modern, Centre Pompidou, and The Menil Collection.
Retrospectives. Two career retrospectives at the Guggenheim (1971, 2012: “Choices”) and MOCA Los Angeles (1986).
Market. Auction record of $5.53M (Nutcracker, Sotheby's 2018) — and still climbing: on May 14, 2026, Sotheby's sold Jo-So (1960) for $1.98M, 98% over its high estimate — sustained, accelerating demand well beyond the record lot.

2027 Centennial Programming

IRL: Six ways to bring the centennial to life.

Estate Dinners. Bring tastemakers and opinion leaders together at the Chamberlain estate for private, curated dinners. Create the moment that echoes throughout culture organically and cannot be escaped on social media.
Performances. Acoustic sets and intimate concerts with musicians known for their affinity for art and for Chamberlain's work specifically.
Talks & Workshops. Conversations and workshops with visual artists in dialogue with Chamberlain's practice — Daniel Arsham, Samuel Ross, and others, American and international.
Auto Culture. Cars & Coffee meetups and auto shows with influential local collections and clubs, honoring Chamberlain's crushed-steel origins.
Family & Education. Hands-on programming for the next generation — build-your-own-Chamberlain foil sculpture workshops and other accessible arts education.
Pop-Up Commerce. Limited-run shops and sales activations at the estate, museum exhibitions, partner retail locations, and major art fairs throughout the year — bringing centennial editions and collectibles to where the audience already gathers.

The precedent already exists

Chase already runs this playbook: Sapphire Reserve cardholders get behind-the-scenes access to Formula E via Jaguar TCS Racing, Sundance, and Apple Originals premieres, plus an exclusive OpenTable dining program.

Competitively, Amex Platinum offers presale and "By Invitation Only" cultural events, and Soho House has built member loyalty around an in-house art collection and "In Conversations" talks series.

Chamberlain's centennial gives Chase an owned, authentic art-world moment — not a licensed placement — anchored in an unmistakably American material story that speaks directly to its audience.

We've Done This Before

This isn't a hypothetical model — it's one we've run before. Daniel Arsham x StockX's "Art & Residence" curated a Milan Design Week exhibition alongside a rolling slate of DropX™ exclusive releases — apparel, collectible furniture, even a 4-unit auctioned racing jacket. Same playbook: one artist, many partners, a sustained calendar, and a story bigger than a logo placement.
